Authenticity Assurance

How We Guarantee the Authenticity of Every Bag

At Bella Boutique, authenticity is not just a promise — it is a core part of who we are. Here is how we ensure every bag meets the highest standards of originality.

01

Verification Process

When you request a bag, we provide clear photos and detailed videos upon request.

After you confirm your interest, each bag undergoes a thorough inspection by a trusted third-party specialist in luxury product authentication.

This expert team follows strict criteria, examining materials, stitching, serial numbers, and brand-specific details.

Once the bag passes inspection, an official authenticity certificate is issued.

02

Serial Number

Every genuine luxury bag carries its own unique serial number assigned by the original manufacturer.

We provide this serial number to you as an additional layer of transparency and confidence.

03

Optional Additional Certificates

For clients seeking an extra level of verification, we offer optional authentication certificates from globally recognized services such as Entrupy or LegitGrails.

This service is optional and comes with an additional fee paid by the client.

04

Refund Policy

We guarantee a full refund within 7 days of receiving the bag if it is proven to be non-authentic.

Your trust is our top priority.

Every bag we offer is chosen, inspected, and documented with the same care we would want for ourselves.
