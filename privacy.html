Privacy Policy

Your Privacy, Respected

We collect as little as possible. No accounts, no tracking pixels, no advertising networks — only what is needed to run the shop and answer your messages.

Last updated: August 2026

01

What We Collect

Browsing this site does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number to look around.

Our server keeps aggregate visit counts only — how many pages were opened each day, and which pages. These are plain numbers.

We do not store IP addresses, and we do not build a profile of you.

02

Cookies

We use two small cookies, and neither carries anything about you personally:

  • bb_vid — a random identifier so a returning device is not counted twice. Contains no personal information.
  • bb_day — records today's date so a single device counts once per day.

No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are used on this site. You may block or delete these cookies in your browser at any time; the site will work normally.

03

When You Contact Us

Orders and enquiries are handled over WhatsApp. When you message us, we receive whatever you choose to send — usually your name, phone number and shipping address.

We use this only to answer you, arrange the sale, and deliver the order. The conversation itself is held on WhatsApp and governed by its own privacy policy.

Payment is arranged directly with you. This website does not process or store any card or bank details.

04

Who Else Is Involved

A few services are needed to put this site on your screen:

  • Railway — hosts the site and delivers its pages.
  • Cloudflare — routes the domain and protects it.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr — supply the typefaces. Loading them means your browser contacts their servers.
  • WhatsApp — carries our conversation when you message us.
  • Saudi Business Center — supplies the verified-store seal shown at the bottom of the page.

We never sell, rent or trade your information, and we share it with no one beyond what delivering your order requires.

05

How Long We Keep Things

Visit counts are aggregate numbers with nothing personal in them, so they are kept as long as the shop runs.

Order details are kept only as long as needed to complete the sale and honour any return, and to meet record-keeping duties under Saudi law.

06

Your Rights

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) you may ask us to:

  • tell you what information about you we hold;
  • correct anything inaccurate;
  • delete your information, where no legal duty requires us to keep it;
  • stop using it for any purpose you did not intend.

Message us on WhatsApp and we will respond. If you are not satisfied, you may raise the matter with the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).

07

Children

This shop is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

08

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the revised version appears on this page with a new date above. Please check back from time to time.

We ask for as little as possible.

No accounts, no advertising networks, no profiles. Only what is needed to show you a bag and get it safely to your hands.
