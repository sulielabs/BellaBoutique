Authenticity & Verification

Verified before it reaches you.

Verification Steps

  1. Sourcing & Intake

    Pieces are received only from trusted suppliers, with provenance reviewed upon arrival.

  2. Detail Inspection

    Hardware, stitching, date codes, leather, and lining are examined according to each brand’s standards.

  3. Independent Authentication

    A certified third-party luxury authenticator inspects every piece and issues an official certificate.

  4. Documented & Listed

    Condition is graded accurately and photographed fully before the item is listed.
